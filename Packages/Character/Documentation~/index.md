# Spellbound.Character

A character's body and rig for a Unity game.

## Assemblies

- `Spellbound.Character`: `CharacterKit`, the front door.
- `Spellbound.Character.Body`: the capsule, the support probe, the movement maths, the camera seam, the reference frame binding and the playable marker.
- `Spellbound.Character.Rig`: the animation seam, the rig contract and the ragdoll builder.
- `Spellbound.Character.Cinemachine`: the camera rig over Cinemachine, compiled only when Cinemachine is installed.

## Samples

- Locomotion: walk, run, jump and fall over StateMachine.
